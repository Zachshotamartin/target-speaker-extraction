## Validation diagnostics

Both websites also present four descriptive views derived from the saved full
validation rows, without retraining or new inference:

- **Quality spread:** complete 2 dB histograms, including negative improvements,
  for the selected epoch 80 and the latest evaluated epoch 81.
- **Failure rates:** full-validation no-improvement and wrong-speaker rates
  through training. Wrong speaker means interferer SI-SDR exceeds target SI-SDR
  by more than 3 dB. These two failure groups overlap and must not be summed.
- **By speaker:** mean improvement for each of 40 development speakers, ordered
  by epoch-80 mean, plus that checkpoint's 25th–75th request percentiles. These
  intervals describe case variation, not statistical confidence. Group sizes
  range from 63 to 223 requests.
- **Best versus latest validation:** 6,000 requests paired by case identity,
  showing score changes between epochs. The ±1 dB buckets are descriptive and
  do not imply statistical significance.

The best model improves 5,698/6,000 requests; 302 do not improve and 241 meet the
wrong-speaker criterion. Median improvement is 13.381 dB and the tenth
percentile is 7.257 dB. Between the two evaluated checkpoints, 391 requests gain
more than 1 dB, 452 lose more than 1 dB, and 5,157 stay within ±1 dB.

Downloadable SVGs, per-request/per-speaker/history CSVs, and a complete JSON
report include source-file hashes. The small imported summary keeps the raw
evaluation data out of the JavaScript bundle. Reproduce these artifacts with
`scripts/publish_validation_diagnostics.py --best <best-full-validation.json>
--latest <latest-full-validation.json> --metrics <metrics.jsonl> --site site`.
The generator excludes monitor and newer history entries, checks cohort
identity, and validates aggregate scores against the saved rows. As throughout
this release, epoch-81 validation is nine updates older than the latest saved
checkpoint; it is never assigned to those newer weights.
