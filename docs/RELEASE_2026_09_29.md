# September 29 model snapshot

Training is **paused and resumable**, after 81 of the planned 100 epochs
(281,484 of 347,500 optimizer updates). The schedule was not completed, and no
automatic convergence or early-stopping criterion was triggered. The same
random-initialized, three-block BSRNN run used 13,900 Libri2Mix train-100 mixtures
and 251 speakers. No additional training was performed for this publication.

| Measurement | Update | Epoch | Mean SI-SDR improvement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Best full development validation, 6,000 requests | 278,000 | 80 | 11.7770 dB |
| Latest full development validation, 6,000 requests | 281,475 | 81 | 11.7460 dB |
| Best speaker-balanced monitor, 400 requests | 238,000 | 68.4892 | 11.8437 dB |

The two suites must not be interchanged. Both use 40 development speakers
excluded from training. These results are development measurements used for
selection, not a pristine held-out test, transcription accuracy, or proof of
noise-free speech. The full-development best still improved at epoch 80 even
though the smaller monitor's best had not improved since update 238,000.
